## 🔬 About Me

I am an **Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE)** researcher specializing in **renewable energy systems, solar photovoltaics (PV), green hydrogen production, photoelectronic device simulation, and applied artificial intelligence/machine learning**.

* 🏛️ **Academic Background**: B.Sc. in EEE (Major in Electronics) at **University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB)** with a **3.87 / 4.00 CGPA**.
* 🇬🇧 **Research Experience**: Numerical Simulation of Photoelectronic Devices Intern at **King's College London (KCL), UK**, simulating PEC solar-to-hydrogen devices using COMSOL Multiphysics.
* 📚 **Publication Track Record**:
  * **3× Q1 Journal Publications** (including *Energy Conversion and Management*, IF: 10.9, and *IEEE Access*, IF: 3.6).
  * **4× Scopus-Indexed IEEE Conference Proceedings** (ICPS, INFOTEH, CIEES, BITCON).
* 🎯 **Research Interests**: Solar PV Harvesting | Green Hydrogen | Net-Zero Carbon | AI & Deep Learning for Microgrids | Semiconductor Physics.
